01 · About Me

Building interfaces that feel obvious in hindsight.

I'm a fourth-year UI/UX design student at Symbiosis Institute of Design (SID), Nagpur, deeply interested in how micro-interactions, structure, and clarity create effortless digital experiences.

Design should be invisible when it works. I blend thorough user research, modular design systems, and rapid prototyping to solve real product challenges.

My process usually starts with research and messy sketches before anything touches Figma. I care deeply about accessibility, scalable design systems, and interfaces that respect a user's time. Outside of coursework, I like picking apart digital products I use daily and asking why their patterns were chosen.

Currently focusing on product thinking, design tokens, responsive web interfaces, and complex application flows.
