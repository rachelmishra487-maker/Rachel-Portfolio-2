Personal Journey & Approach

Hi, I'm Rachel Mishra.

Fourth-Year UI/UX Designer · SID Nagpur

Hi! I'm Rachel, a final-year UI/UX design student at Symbiosis Institute of Design (SID), Nagpur.

To me, great design isn't just about clean visuals—it's about understanding why people get stuck, what causes hesitation, and how an interface can feel effortless and calm. I love digging into messy, real-world problems—whether that's exploring how to ease tech burnout for hospital doctors or helping international students navigate complex university applications—and shaping those insights into clear user journeys, scalable Figma design systems, and tactile prototypes.

During my UI/UX Design internship at Livyana Fashion & Arts (completed July 2026), I designed and prototyped responsive web platforms and digital campaigns. Working directly with developers and project stakeholders gave me hands-on experience taking ideas from rough wireframes all the way to launch while keeping user needs at the center.

Outside of design tools, I'm energized by dancing, discovering new music, and long highway drives. As I head toward graduation, I am actively seeking UI/UX Design internship opportunities starting January 2027 where I can collaborate with thoughtful product teams, learn from seasoned mentors, and build experiences that truly make a difference.
