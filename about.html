01 · My Story & Approach

Hi, I'm Rachel Mishra.

Fourth-Year UI/UX Design Student & Product Thinker

I believe the best digital experiences are the ones that feel second nature—effortless, transparent, and built with genuine empathy. As a fourth-year UI/UX design student at Symbiosis Institute of Design (SID), Nagpur, I'm fascinated by human psychology: uncovering how people make decisions, where they experience friction, and how thoughtful design can turn complex workflows into calm, intuitive interactions.

My design practice spans the entire product journey, with a core focus on UX research to uncover real user needs, mapping clear user flows and information architecture, crafting tactile, accessible UI design, and validating interactions through high-fidelity prototyping. I have a particular obsession with design systems—establishing design tokens, reusable component libraries, and scalable guidelines that empower teams to build cohesively.

As I approach graduation, I am currently looking for UI/UX & Product Design internship opportunities where I can collaborate with forward-thinking teams, learn from seasoned design mentors, and contribute to meaningful, real-world digital products.
