Personal Journey & Approach

Hi, I'm Rachel Mishra.

Fourth-Year UI/UX Designer · SID Nagpur

I believe that exceptional interfaces should feel second nature—calm, transparent, and built with genuine empathy for how real people think, feel, and make decisions under cognitive load.

As a final-year UI/UX design student at Symbiosis Institute of Design (SID), Nagpur, my work bridges qualitative human research with rigorous interface craft. I enjoy immersing myself in ambiguous problem spaces—whether that involves untangling doctor burnout in hospital wards or simplifying complex cross-border university applications—and translating raw user friction into structured user journeys, accessible design systems, and tactile, high-fidelity prototypes.

Recently, during my UI/UX Design internship at Livyana Fashion & Arts, I helped design and prototype responsive web platforms and digital campaigns. The experience strengthened my ability to balance user empathy with brand storytelling and front-end feasibility.

As I prepare to graduate, I am actively seeking UI/UX Design internship opportunities starting January 2027 where I can collaborate with thoughtful product teams, learn from seasoned design mentors, and contribute to products that make a tangible difference.
