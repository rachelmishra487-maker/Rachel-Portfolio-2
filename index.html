About

I'm a fourth-year UI/UX design student at Symbiosis Institute of Design, Nagpur, interested in how small interaction details change whether a product feels effortless or exhausting.

My process usually starts with research and messy sketches before anything touches Figma. I care about accessibility, clean design systems, and interfaces that respect a user's time. Outside of coursework, I like picking apart apps I use daily and asking why they were built that way.
