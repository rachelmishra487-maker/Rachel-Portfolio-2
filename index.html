Design Philosophy & Story

Hi, I'm Rachel Mishra.

Fourth-Year UI/UX Designer · SID Nagpur

I believe that exceptional interfaces should feel second nature—calm, transparent, and built with genuine empathy for how real people think, feel, and make decisions under cognitive load.

As a final-year UI/UX design student at Symbiosis Institute of Design (SID), Nagpur, my work bridges qualitative human research with rigorous interface craft. I enjoy immersing myself in ambiguous problem spaces—whether untangling doctor burnout in hospital wards or simplifying complex cross-border university applications—and translating raw user friction into structured user journeys, accessible design systems, and tactile, high-fidelity prototypes.

During my UI/UX Design internship at Livyana Fashion & Arts (completed July 2026), I helped design and prototype responsive web platforms and digital campaigns, balancing user empathy with brand storytelling and front-end feasibility.
